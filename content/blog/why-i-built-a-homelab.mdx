Reading about Linux administration only goes so far. I wanted an environment where breaking something didn't mean breaking production, so I put together a small homelab on repurposed hardware.

## The Setup

I started with Proxmox as a hypervisor, split the network into VLANs for management and services, and put an Nginx reverse proxy in front of everything self-hosted.

## What Broke First

The first real lesson came from a botched firewall rule that locked me out of SSH entirely — a good reminder to always test firewall changes from a console session, not just over the network you're about to cut off.

## What I'd Tell Past Me

Document configuration changes as you make them, even if it feels unnecessary at the time. Six months later, "why is this rule here" is a much harder question to answer without notes.
