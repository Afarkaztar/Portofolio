Making it to the finalist round of a national CTF competition was the first time competition pressure really tested how I work under a deadline, not just what I know.

## Preparation

In the weeks leading up to finals, I focused on categories I was weakest in — forensics and reverse engineering — rather than practicing more of what I already knew well.

## During the Competition

Time management mattered more than raw skill. Logging quick wins early built momentum and bought time to sit with the harder challenges later.

## What's Next

I'm building a personal toolkit of scripts and notes so the next competition starts from a stronger baseline instead of relearning the same recon steps.
